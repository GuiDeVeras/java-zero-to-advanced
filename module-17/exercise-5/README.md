Exercise 5
	In an online course portal, each user has a unique code, represented by an integer.
	Each instructor on the portal can have several courses, and a single student can enroll in as many courses as they want. Thus, the total number of students for an instructor is not simply the sum of the students in all the courses they have, as there may be students who are repeated in more than one course.
	Instructor Alex has three courses A, B, and C, and wants to know his total number of students.

	Your program should read the students from instructor Alex's courses A, B, and C, then show the total number of students, as in the example.
Example:

How many students for course A? 3
21
35
22
How many students for course B? 2
21
50
How many students for course C? 3
42
35
13
Total students: 6
